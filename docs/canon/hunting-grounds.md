# Hunting grounds

Part of the Lineage II canon (Interlude, C6): the reference the game takes names and numbers from.
It is not a roadmap. Read [README.md](README.md) before using an entry.

Where each ground is, how it is reached, its level band and its monsters by level. Monster stats beyond name and level (HP, attack, defence, EXP, Adena) are in [monsters.md](monsters.md) for the four villages' grounds and Ruins of Despair; past those, see the gaps in [README.md](README.md).

## Disputes and caveats

What the checks could not settle, kept as the checker wrote it.

- How this was checked: all 348 monster name and level pairs in the original data were compared, by script, with an independent source that the researcher did not use: an old L2J Interlude npc.sql (github.com/david-buzas/l2j-interlude-datapack). Every name matched exactly and every level matched, including the two-id cases (Gremlin, Mandragora Sprout, and Ol Mahum Captain at 25 and 30). Gatekeeper names (Roxxy, Mirabel, Jasmine, Tamil, Richlin, Bella, Trisha) and teleport names and prices were checked against Mobius's own teleporter XML (data/teleporters/town/*.xml), which is a separate file from the Mobius spawns the researcher used, and against old L2J teleport.sql.
- Corrected: the Windawood Manor teleport costs 1,400 Adena, not 1,700. Mobius, aCis and old L2J all agree.
- Corrected: Evil Hunting Grounds, School of Dark Arts ('Center of the School of Dark Arts') and Immortal Plateau, Northern Region are all on their village gatekeepers' NOBLESSE teleport lists in Mobius, aCis and old L2J. The original said they were not on any gatekeeper list, or that their names came only from aCis admin data. All three are now confirmed-2-sources.
- Renamed: 'Langk Lizardmen Dwellings' is now 'Langk Lizardman Dwellings', which is how the gatekeeper writes it in both Mobius and aCis.
- Upgraded: Dion Hills, Maille Lizardman Barracks and Partisan's Hideaway are now confirmed as names by quest dialogue in old L2J (quests 380 and 213, plus the quest HTML). The original had them from aCis only. Partisan's Hideaway stays single-source because which monsters belong there is inferred. I added the level-30 Ol Mahum Captain to it on the strength of the quest text.
- Talking Island, Eastern Territory remains uncertain. The name appears only in aCis, in its admin teleport and in a spawn-file header. That header labels region 16_24, which is the north-west of the island where the gatekeeper's 'Western Territory (Northern Area)' teleport lands, while the admin teleport points into 17_25. Old L2J and Mobius never use the name. This conflict was not reported in the original.
- l2db.ru's 'interlude' teleport page is NOT clean Interlude data: it lists Kamael Island and Kamael Village destinations, which came after Interlude, and the Gludin entry has wrong prices for Dark Elf Village and Dwarven Village (26,000 and 46,000, where the Mobius and aCis gatekeepers say 16,000 and 38,000). Do not treat it as a primary source for prices or spellings. Its 'Dark Elven Village', 'Langk Lizardman Dwelling' and 'Fellmere Hunting Grounds' spellings probably come from other chronicles or internal labels.
- Linedia (linedia.ru) now answers every request, API included, with a reCAPTCHA page, so none of the zone-name claims attributed to Linedia could be re-checked. Every entry that keeps its confidence has at least two other datapack or quest-text sources.
- Spelling variants that are still open, because the client's zonename file could not be read: Wasteland or Wastelands; Execution Grounds or Execution Ground; Bee Hive or Beehive; Fellmere Harvesting Grounds or Fellmere Harvest Grounds; Partisan's Hideaway or Partisan Hideaway; Immortal Plateau, Northern Region or The Immortal Plateau. Each entry uses the spelling that the gatekeeper or the quest text gives, and its facts list the alternatives.
- The monster groupings in Cave of Trials, Fellmere Harvesting Grounds, Red Rock Ridge, Evil Hunting Grounds, Dion Hills, Floran Agricultural Area, School of Dark Arts and Partisan's Hideaway are inferred from 32,768-unit spawn regions that hold more than one named area (see the aCis spawnlist headers). The names are verified; which zone each monster belongs to is not.
- Nothing was dropped: no entry turned out to be invented or from the wrong chronicle. No entries were added. Iris Lake, Black Rock Hill, Fellmere Lake, The Ruined Bend, Frozen Waterfall (a Tamil teleport, 1,600 Adena), Valley of Heroes and Fortress of Resistance exist in the data, but their monsters could not be separated cleanly from their neighbours'.
- Two new independent checks were added. (1) Teleport names and prices were compared with the official Interlude PTS gatekeeper AI scripts, ported in dr3dd/L2Interlude (Core/Module/NpcAi/Ai/NpcTeleporter: Clavier = Giran, Valentina = Oren, Elisabeth = Aden, Esmeralda = Hunters Village, Flauen = Heine, Tatiana = Goddard, Ilyana = Rune). Every regular-teleport name and Adena price the researcher gave matched exactly.
