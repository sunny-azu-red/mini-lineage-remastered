### What the healing potions do (researched 2026-10-12)

- **Lesser Healing Potion (1060)** triggers skill 2031-1: a heal over time of 16 HP every 2 seconds
  for 7 ticks, 112 HP over 15 seconds (L2J and aCis; the retail-format PTS data gives `t_hp;8;3`
  for 15 seconds, so 8 HP a second, 120, is the unresolved alternative). Reuse 10 seconds (aCis on
  item and skill, PTS on the item); L2J has none but refuses a second heal over time for about 10
  seconds. No use time, usable in combat, stackable in one slot. Sold by every starting village's
  grocer (Katerina, Herbiel, Vollodos, Papuma, Mion) at 103 and by Harmony in Gludio at 108.
- **Healing Potion (1061)** triggers 2032-1, 48 HP every 2 seconds × 7, 336 HP; 10 seconds' reuse.
  Sold only in Gludio (Harmony, 396), never in a starting village. The second Healing Potion (727)
  heals only in L2J; aCis and PTS give it no skill.
- **Greater Healing Potion (1539)**, 100 × 7 = 700 HP, and **Quick Healing Potion (1540)**, an
  instant 435 HP with a 0.5 second reuse, are sold by no NPC for Adena (Seven Signs priests and
  castle blacksmiths).
- **Mana Potion (728)** is sold by no NPC. Its effect disagrees: L2J an instant 400 MP, aCis 18% of
  most MP with a 2 second reuse, PTS nothing.
- A stronger heal over time replaces a weaker; the same potion refreshes itself (PTS
  `abnormal_type=life_force`, aCis stack orders 1, 2, 3).

## Enchant rules

- **Safe enchant limit**: Items enchant safely to +3; one-piece (full-body) armour is safe to +4. Past +3, armour gains +3 P.Def per level instead of +1. No-grade items have no scroll, so they cannot be enchanted. Sources: [l2db.ru](https://l2db.ru/items/other/scroll/interlude/), [strategywiki.org](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Lineage_II/Items), [github:david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server/main/src/main/resources/config/other.properties).
- **Failure with a normal scroll**: Failing past the safe limit crystallises the item: it is destroyed and returns crystals of its grade. Sources: [strategywiki.org](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Lineage_II/Items), [github:david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server/main/src/main/java/net/sf/l2j/gameserver/clientpackets/RequestEnchantItem.java).
- **Failure with a Blessed scroll**: The item is not crystallised; its enchant drops back to +0. Blessed scrolls were Olympiad-shop items in Interlude. Sources: [strategywiki.org](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Lineage_II/Items), [github:david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server/main/src/main/java/net/sf/l2j/gameserver/clientpackets/RequestEnchantItem.java), [l2db.ru](https://l2db.ru/items/other/scroll/interlude/).
- **Enchant success rate** **(uncertain: check again before use)**: StrategyWiki says 70% per over-enchant step. The L2J emulator config uses weapon 68, armour 52, jewellery 54. No official NCSoft figure was found. Sources: [strategywiki.org](https://strategywiki.org/wiki/Lineage_II/Items), [github:david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/david-buzas/l2j-interlude-game-server/main/src/main/resources/config/other.properties).

## Disputes and caveats

What the checks could not settle, kept as the checker wrote it.

- Independent cross-check: I compared every jewellery and consumable row against the aCis Interlude datapack (github DEVjEXTREME/L2jaCis-382, data/xml/items), which the original researcher did not use. All 113 graded jewels and 8 epic jewels match exactly on id, name, M.Def, base price, grade and MP bonus. aCis comes from the same L2J family, so it is not fully independent of the L2J source, but it was curated separately (its drop tables differ, for example).
- Corrections to the original. (1) Epic drop chances: L2J's 30% for Queen Ant, Core and Orfen conflicts with aCis (100%, about 55% and 100%). Both are emulator values, so I removed the specific chances. (2) Frintezza's Necklace dropper is now two-source: aCis gives Scarlet van Halisha (npc 29047, lvl 85) at 100%, which agrees with PMfun. (3) Necklace of Valakas 'HP +445' is only in l2db.ru's item text; neither datapack has it as a stat. (4) Elixir of Mental Strength reuse: PMfun says 5 minutes, but L2J's skill 2288 uses 9 minutes (540000 ms). This is unresolved.
- Added entries the original only mentioned in passing or left out: Haste Potion (734, and a second id 1062), Potion of Alacrity (735), Greater Haste Potion (1374), Greater Swift Attack Potion (1375), Magic Haste Potion (6035) and Greater Magic Haste Potion (6036), Scroll of Resurrection (737) and Blessed Scroll of Resurrection (3936), and prices for the graded elixirs.
- The herb series is now confirmed in two datapacks, including the odd 'Herb of Critical Attack.' with a trailing period. That spelling may be a datapack artefact rather than the retail client string; it was not checked against a client dump.
- dropped: nothing. Every name the original gave was found in at least two sources. The only entry left as 'uncertain' is the Crystal Scrolls, plus the enchant success-rate rule.
- Unsealing bonuses for Sealed A/S jewels (for example M.Def +4 and MP +26 for Phoenix Necklace) are reduced to the stats of the unsealed item, which both datapacks confirm. The sealed item itself has no MP bonus.
- Price conflicts that remain: l2db.ru against both datapacks for Greater Healing Potion (900 vs 1,080), CP Potion (200 vs 240), Greater CP Potion (500 vs 600), the Blessed enchant scrolls (l2db.ru far higher) and the Crystal scrolls (l2db.ru 0). These are client reference prices, not shop prices.
- Not reachable or not useful: lineage2.fandom, l2wiki.com and l2central returned 403 to the earlier researcher. The l2db.ru detail page for Elixir 8622 shows no description, and a web search found no other Interlude source for the elixir text, so the 'below level 19' wording rests on PMfun, which is a later-chronicle site. Its use as Interlude text is plausible but single-source.
- Kept as real game spellings: 'Soulshot: D-grade' (lowercase g), 'Blessed Spiritshot: D-Grade' (uppercase G), 'Blessed Spiritshot: S Grade' (no hyphen), 'Elixir of Life (D-Grade)', 'Food For Wolves' and 'Food For Hatchling' next to 'Food for Strider' and 'Food for Wyvern'.
