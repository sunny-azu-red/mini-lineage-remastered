# Towns, Gatekeepers and the Newbie Guide

Part of the Lineage II canon (Interlude, C6): the reference the game takes names and numbers from.
It is not a roadmap. Read [README.md](README.md) before using an entry.

Every town's people, every Gatekeeper's destinations and fees, the temples, and the free help a new character gets. A fee is the Gatekeeper's base price.

## Disputes and caveats

What the checks could not settle, kept as the checker wrote it.

- I added two Interlude datapacks the researcher did not use: david-buzas/l2j-interlude-datapack and mars8022/L2jFrozen. They supplied npc.sql, spawnlist.sql, teleport.sql, gatekeeper html, helper_buff_list, skills and etcitem. Both are L2J-derived, so they are not fully independent of Hl4p3x L2J C6. I did not try any wikis.
- All 16 gatekeeper destination lists and prices match in the two new packs. The researcher's PTS/aCis figures hold, apart from the disputes noted.
- Newbie buff ranges are confirmed by these packs' helper_buff_list (8-24, 11-23, 12-22, 13-21, 14-20, 15-19, 16-19). Their skill names, all ending 'for Beginners', match in both 4300-4399.xml files.
- Price dispute: Aden -> Seal of Shilen is 3000 in PTS and aCis but 3300 in both L2J-derived packs. Dion -> Tanor Canyon is 3900 in PTS and aCis but 3800 in the L2J packs. The PTS value is given in each entry.
- Gludin -> Dark Elf Village (16000) is in all three L2J-derived packs but not in PTS or aCis, so it is still disputed.
- Label disputes remain: 'Giran Castle Town' (PTS) vs 'The Town of Giran'. For the 960-Adena Orc Village entry, PTS says 'The Immortal Plateau, Northern Region', the L2J packs say 'The Immortal Plateau', and the L2J packs call the 2000 entry 'Immortal Plateau, Southern Region', without 'The'. 'Frozen Labyrinth' (PTS and L2J) vs 'Ice Merchant Cabin' (aCis). 'Langk Lizardman Dwelling' (L2J html) vs 'Dwellings' (PTS and aCis).
- Every town NPC name and title in the entries was found in david-buzas npc.sql, including O'Fulle (31572) and Klaus Blackbird (30187). Many titles in that file have a trailing space ('Spellbook Seller ', 'Hierarch ', 'High Prefect '); that is datapack noise, not part of the name.
- Spellbook Seller Burns (31670) is spawned in Hunters Village in both new spawnlists under a retail-style territory name, which counts weakly toward it being retail. aCis lacks it, so it stays uncertain.
- Spellbook Seller Raheel (31973) and the Schuttgart second masters (32092-32098) have no spawn in the two new spawnlists. They are unconfirmed for Interlude, and I left them out of the confirmed rosters.
- Newbie Travel Token (8542) is in L2jFrozen's etcitem.sql but missing from the older david-buzas etcitem.sql. The name is confirmed by Frozen plus the researcher's PTS and aCis sources.
- Not re-verified: spawn positions for every roster NPC (only the Newbie Guides, Burns, Raheel and Arisha/Stanislava were checked), the shop roles derived from buy lists, and the PTS 'PrimeHours' half-price list.
- No entries dropped: none of the names could be shown to be invented or from the wrong chronicle.
- Orc trainers' menu label: "Learn skills" in all three sources for Brukurse, Karukia and Livina. Tanapi's reads "Learn a skill." in L2J and aCis and "Learn Skills" in PTS. The two packs also word Tanapi's and Livina's greetings differently from PTS ("Kasha's Evil Spirit" vs "spirit of Kasha"; L2J writes "Pa'agrio" where PTS writes "Paagrio").
