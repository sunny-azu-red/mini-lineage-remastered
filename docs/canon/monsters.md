# Monsters

Part of the Lineage II canon (Interlude, C6): the reference the game takes names and numbers from.
It is not a roadmap. Read [README.md](README.md) before using an entry.

Every monster around the four starting villages and on Gludio's Ruins of Despair, grouped by the
area `hunting-grounds.md` puts it in. **The numbers are L2J C6's** (`npc.sql` and `droplist.sql`):
two L2J Interlude packs agree on every one, and aCis 409 agrees on level, attributes, MP, EXP, SP,
aggression, skills and the Adena line. l2hub.info, the independent check, agrees on level, HP and
attributes but often differs on Adena, EXP and SP past level 20, MEN and Atk. Spd.; each entry's
notes give its figures. An entry marked single source is one where l2hub disagrees.

How a monster's other stats are worked out, in both L2J and aCis: **Accuracy and Evasion are
√DEX × 6 + level**, as a player's are, and **Critical starts from a base of 4** (L2J leaves it at
4%; aCis multiplies it by the DEX bonus). Sources for that are under "How monsters are worked out".

## How monsters are worked out

- **NPC Accuracy (L2J C6 and aCis)**: Monsters compute Accuracy the same way players do, from DEX and level, with no fixed base and no weapon bonus: L2J C6 gives every NPC the shared NPC_STD_CALCULATOR (Creature.java L260-263), whose ACCURACY_COMBAT calculator is FuncAtkAccuracy (Formulas.java L337-380, sqrt(DEX)*6 + level), and aCis adds FuncAtkAccuracy to every Creature in addFuncsToNewCharacter (Creature.java L186-207; FuncAtkAccuracy.java L23-31, BASE_EVASION_ACCURACY[i] = sqrt(i)*6 at Formulas.java L110). l2hub's IL pages instead show the evasion figure plus a per-NPC modifier of +0, +5 or +9 (presumably PTS's physical hit modifier), e.g. Young Keltir 38 against 33, which neither emulator models. Sources: aCis 409 .../scripting/script/ai/individual/Monster/WarriorBase/Warrior/WarriorCastDDMagic/WarriorCastDDMagic.java.
- **aCis WarriorCast3SkillsMagical2 (DD_MAGIC1 + DD_MAGIC2 + DEBUFF: e.g. Dre Vanul Disposer 20056 with 4001 lv2 + 4002 lv2)** **(single source)**: Only when the attacker is more than 100 away and is the most-hated: independent 33% rolls to queue DD_MAGIC1, DD_MAGIC2 and (if not already on the target) DEBUFF. In melee it just fights. Sources: aCis 409 .../Monster/WarriorBase/Warrior/WarriorCast3SkillsMagical2/WarriorCast3SkillsMagical2.java (Aggressive subclass lists 20056, 20117).
- **aCis WizardDDMagic2 (W_LONG_RANGE_DD_MAGIC 4151/4152 + W_SHORT_RANGE_DD_MAGIC 4160: e.g. Monster Eye 20092, Evil Eye Watcher 20427)** **(single source)**: A spam caster: on being hit (80% if the attacker is over 100 away, else always) it queues the long-range nuke beyond 100 range or Aura Burn within 100, and onUseSkillFinished re-queues the next spell immediately on the most-hated target. It casts back-to-back until MP runs short, then sets _i_ai0=1 and melees for the rest of the fight. This is why aCis gives the '- Magic' skills no reuse and low MP (official MP: 4151 lv1 7, 4152 lv1 9, 4160 3). Sources: aCis 409 .../Monster/WizardBase/Wizard/WizardDDMagic2/WizardDDMagic2.java.
- **aCis WarriorSlowType / 1 / 2 (DD_MAGIC_SLOW: 4247, 4248, 4253, 4254 on golems and zombies)** **(single source)**: Base: a 5000 ms timer starts on the first hit, and each tick in which the most-hated did not hit it, 50% chance to queue DD_MAGIC_SLOW; clan members not yet fighting 10%. Type1 (e.g. Stone Golem 20016, Greystone Golem 20333): additionally 10% per hit when the attacker is within 200. Type2 (e.g. Marsh Zombie 20015, Zombie Soldier 20457): 70% per hit when the attacker is more than 200 away. 'Slow' means a ranged poke from a slow melee mob, not a Slow debuff. Sources: aCis 409 .../Monster/WarriorBase/Warrior/WarriorSlowType/WarriorSlowType.java, WarriorSlowType1.java, WarriorSlowType2.java.
- **Magic damage formula (both emulators)**: MDAM and DRAIN damage = 91 * sqrt(M.Atk) / M.Def * power (x2/x4 for spiritshot/blessed, x4 on a magic crit in aCis), times the elemental modifier. lvlDepend and magicLvl only enter the success/resist roll (aCis calcMagicSuccess, getLevelModifier), never damage. Sources: L2J C6 java/org/l2jserver/gameserver/model/skills/Formulas.java line ~1748, aCis 409 aCis_gameserver/.../skills/Formulas.java calcMagicDam() line ~596.

## Disputes and caveats

What the checks could not settle, kept as the checker wrote it.

- Stats are the L2J C6 npc.sql template (identical in the david-buzas and Hl4p3x packs for all 44 monsters), which is also what an L2J server shows in-game since monsters get no attribute or level modifiers there. level, HP, EXP, SP, STR/CON/DEX/INT/WIT and cast speed match l2hub IL exactly for every monster; EXP is l2hub's figure floored (l2hub shows fractions such as 35.268).
- MP: L2J's figure matches l2hub's C5 and CT1 pages, but in-game IL MP is about x1.1 (MEN bonus), which aCis computes and l2hub IL displays (Young Keltir 44 vs 48). MEN: L2J, aCis and l2hub C5 say 10; l2hub IL shows 20 for every monster, which does not fit its own MP figures and looks like a display default.
- M.Atk and M.Def rarely agree across sources: L2J stores a finished number, aCis applies INT/MEN bonuses and PTS modifier passives (4411/4413), and l2hub IL shows a third figure. Treat M.Atk and M.Def as L2J-only unless an entry says otherwise. Stone Golem and Crasher's M.Def is doubled in-game by a passive in every source.
- Atk. Spd: l2hub IL shows 278 for every monster. L2J's 249 for bow users and 317 for skeletons and wererats is aCis's base attack speed scaled the same way (221.61 and 282.13 against 247.42), so those are kept. aCis's own in-game value multiplies by DEX_BONUS 1.10, giving 272/243/310.
- Archers' P.Atk: L2J 41 and aCis-derived 40 against l2hub IL 18 (l2hub probably leaves out the bow). Dungeon Skeleton, Dread Soldier and Sukar Wererat P.Atk: aCis-derived and l2hub agree above L2J (29, 36, 52-54 against 26, 32, 46), so those three are marked uncertain.
- Adena: L2J (both packs), aCis 409 and l2hub's C4, C5 and CT1 pages all give the same min, max and chance (70%, or 100% for the tutorial Gremlin). Only l2hub's IL pages show about 0.9 times those amounts (Young Keltir 7-10 against 8-12), which contradicts its own neighbouring chronicles, so the L2J/aCis figure is reported and the l2hub IL figure is in each entry's facts.
- Aggression: L2J's aggro 500 and l2hub IL's aggressive flag and AI class ('warrior_aggressive' and the like) agree on all 13 aggressive monsters. aCis 409 marks every NPC aggroRange 1000 and decides aggression in its AI scripts (only Orc Fighter carries SetAggressiveTime=0), so it was not used for this; l2hub also lists 1000 as everyone's 'agro range'.
- Active skills (L2J npcskills, aCis <skills>, l2hub operateType A) agree for all 44: NPC Might 4028 (Orc Fighter Leader), NPC Windstrike - Slow 4247 (Stone Golem, Crasher), Poison 4035 (Dark Terror), Decrease Speed 4076 (Shade Horror) and Hold 4047 Lv2 (Dre Vanul Tracker). Everything else has passive race, resistance and weakness skills only.
- Choice of ids: Gremlin is 18342 (the tutorial gremlin spawned at Talking Island and every other starting village) because 20001 'Gremlin' has no IL spawns or adena; Bearded Keltir is 20481 rather than the unspawned tutorial copy 18003; Orc Fighter is 20093 rather than the quest copy 27002; Wolf is 20120 rather than the pet 12077.
- aCis numbers come from the DEVjEXTREME/L2jaCis-409 mirror, whose XML rounds some bases (hp 39.0), so its derived HP lands 1 below L2J/l2hub. l2hub is a community site built from client and PTS data and was fetched on 2026-10-11; its IL dataset differs from its C5 and CT1 datasets in the ways above.
- Not found: no retail-confirmed source for IL accuracy and critical modifiers per NPC beyond l2hub's display; no second independent source for the l2hub IL adena reduction.
- Every stat block is the L2J value; david-buzas and Hl4p3x are identical on every field, drop and skill for all 64 monsters. aCis 409 matches them on level, STR/CON/DEX/INT/WIT/MEN, MP, EXP, SP, aggression, attack range, active skills and the whole Adena line, but stores PTS-style base values before attribute bonuses (e.g. Imp Elder HP 108 base vs 171), so it cannot confirm HP/P.Atk/M.Atk/M.Def/Atk.Spd directly (HP and Atk.Spd convert exactly by x1.58 and x1.124).
- l2hub (the independent check) agrees with L2J on npc id, level, HP, Cast.Spd 333, STR/CON/DEX/INT/WIT, aggression, melee/bow, active skills and the 70% Adena chance for all 64. It differs systematically on: MEN (20 everywhere vs 10), MP (about +10%), Adena min/max (about -10% at level 20 and below, about +5% at level 21+; only the Gremlin matches), EXP and SP (x1.2 at level 21+, equal below), Atk.Spd (always 278; L2J has 249 for archers and 317 for dagger/fist users), P.Atk of the two archers (18/57 vs 41/129, l2hub ignores the bow), M.Def of zombies/golems (roughly double in l2hub), and P.Atk/M.Atk/M.Def otherwise off by 1-7.
- confidence 'single-source' here means the number set is agreed by all three L2J-lineage packs but l2hub disagrees on the Adena range and/or EXP/SP; HP, level, attributes, aggression, attack type and skills are still confirmed by l2hub. 'confirmed-2-sources' means l2hub also matches Adena, EXP and SP (only Gremlin).
- l2db.duckdns.org (found by search, TLS-unreachable from here) reportedly shows Imp Elder HP 108 and Adena 51-72 at 70%, i.e. aCis-lineage data, so it does not break the Adena tie.
- Adena chance is the drop chance of Adena (category 0 in L2J at 700000/1000000; aCis CURRENCY category 70% x item 100%). No monster has more than one Adena line.
- Atk.Spd 278 is the unarmed/standard NPC value; non-278 values come from the equipped weapon (rhand). Cast.Spd is 333 for all, and aCis has no cast speed field.
- aCis aggroRange is 1000 for every monster (and l2hub 'Agro Range' 1000) regardless of aggression; aggression comes from the AI class (l2hub classes warrior_aggressive* / wizard_ag*) and L2J aggro 500.
- Where these were placed by spawn: Vaiss Orc through Lesser Succubus Tilfo are School of Dark Arts mobs; Evil Eye Watcher, Slave Skeleton, Grave Keeper, Crypt Horror, Kuruka Ratman(s), Skeleton Longbowman, Undead Slave, Ghost and Ghost Guardian are Cave of Trials mobs (x 15-22k, y -108k to -115k), well east of the Orc Village.
- Canon names map to L2J names exactly; PTS aliases differ (Prairie Keltir = crimson_keltir, Green Fungus = forest_walking_fungus, Cave Fang Spider = cave_poker, Omen Beast = onyx_beast, Lesser Succubus = least_succubus, Scavenger Bat = corpse_eater_bat, Balor Orc Lieutenant = balor_orc_sub_leader, Balor Orc Fighter Leader = balor_orc_leader, Vaiss Orc Warrior Leader = vaiss_orc_lieutenant, Goblin Tomb Raider Leader = goblin_tomb_raider). Every canon level matched every source.
- l2hub.info gives official IL data for power (effect args), cast range and MP split (mp_consume1 initial + mp_consume2), but not hit time, reuse or element; those come from L2J and aCis only. Where those two disagree (reuse on 4151/4152/4160/4247-4254, element on 4247) there is no tie-breaker beyond L2J's own 'SHOULD BE 0' comment on the Slow skills' 8000 reuse.
- MP: aCis matches the official totals on 4001, 4151, 4152, 4160, 4247, 4248, 4253, 4254; L2J copies 4001/4002 tables into the '- Magic' and Slow-drain variants. On 4002 both emulators say 18/29 against official 17/28.
- Neither emulator models the official initial/consume MP split for these skills; mpConsume is the whole cost.
- 4248 'NPC HP Drain - Slow' is typed MDAM in both emulators, so its absorbPart 0.2 is dead data there and it heals nothing; officially it is i_hp_drain 5,20.
- Power is fixed per skill LEVEL; NPC data picks the level (lv1 for NPCs around 10-19, lv2 around 20-29, in the aCis npc tables examined). Nothing scales power with the NPC's own level at cast time.
- L2J's chances are per skill per think, so an NPC with two skills casts something on roughly 1-(0.91^2) = 17% of melee thinks. Rnd.get(100) <= 8 is 9%, assuming Rnd.get(n) returns 0..n-1 (standard in both codebases).
- aCis AI is per NPC class and varies; the classes cited are the ones the sampled NPCs map to, not every class that uses these skills.
